# Pizza Sales Analysis

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes pizza sales data to evaluate overall business performance, understand sales and ordering patterns, and identify differences in product demand. Calculated key performance indicators and examined sales across different days, hours, pizza categories, pizza sizes, and individual products. Using **MySQL** for data preparation and analysis and **Power BI** for interactive visualization.

## Interactive Dashboard

![Pizza Sales Interactive Dashboard](Pizza%20Sales%20Interactive%20Dashboard.gif)

The interactive Power BI dashboard brings the analysis together through KPI cards, trend charts, category and size breakdowns, and best- and worst-selling pizza visualizations.

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate pizza sales performance and identify meaningful patterns in revenue, orders, and product demand.

The analysis focuses on:

- Measuring overall sales performance through key business KPIs.
- Identifying days and hours with higher sales and order activity.
- Understanding how different pizza categories and sizes contribute to total sales.
- Identifying the best- and worst-selling pizzas based on quantity sold.
- Presenting the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard to make the analysis easier to explore.

## Methodology

The analysis followed a structured end-to-end workflow:

1. Data Understanding & Quality Assessment
2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Business Analysis & Investigations
5. Dashboard & Final Summary

## Tools & Skills

**MySQL:** Data Import, Cleaning and Preparation, Aggregate Functions, and Subqueries

**Power BI:** Data Visualization, Interactive Filtering, Dashboard Design


## Results & Business Insights

- **Friday recorded the highest sales**, indicating stronger sales activity on this day compared with the other days shown in the dashboard.

- **Order activity was highest around 12 PM–1 PM and again between approximately 4 PM–8 PM**, highlighting the periods with the greatest order volume.

- **The Classic pizza category contributed the largest share of sales**, making it the leading category by sales contribution.

- **Large-sized pizzas accounted for the largest share of sales among all pizza sizes**, indicating their leading contribution to overall sales.

- **Classic Deluxe Pizza was one of the strongest-selling pizzas by quantity**, while **Brie Carre Pizza ranked among the lowest-selling pizzas by quantity**, showing a difference in product-level demand.
